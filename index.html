Password manager

Everything you need. Nothing you pay for.

The features people pay a yearly subscription for, built in and free.

Zero-knowledge encryption

Logins, cards, notes, Wi-Fi, SSH and API keys are encrypted on your phone. The server only ever stores ciphertext.

Autofill and passkeys

Fills your logins in apps and websites, saves new ones, and works as a passkey provider on Android 14.

Built-in 2FA codes

Keep one-time codes next to the login they protect. Scan the QR code or paste the setup key.

Share safely

Share a copy, or share live with view or edit access. Take access away whenever you want.

Security check-up

Find weak, reused and breached passwords. Analysis runs on your phone; breach checks never send a password.

Make it yours

Pick from 50 avatars and 10 app icon styles. Logins get their service’s icon automatically, and you can choose another.

Updates built in

New versions download on Wi-Fi and are checked against the release signing key before Android installs them.

Easy to switch

Import from Chrome, Firefox, Bitwarden, 1Password, LastPass or Dashlane with a CSV file.

Security centre

Know exactly where you're exposed.

A single score shows how healthy your passwords are, then points you to the weak, reused and old ones. Breach checks compare against known leaks using k-anonymity, so only the first five characters of a hash ever leave your phone.

Sharing

Share a password without sending it in a chat.

Items are encrypted to the other person's key on your phone. Share a one-time copy, or keep it in sync with view or edit access. When you remove someone, the item gets a new key so they can't read what changes next.

Backup and recovery

Lose your phone, not your passwords.

Turn on Google Drive backup and Synapse keeps the latest five encrypted backups in a hidden folder only it can use. Set up a recovery kit and you can reset a forgotten master password, without ever giving us a key to your vault.

Made for you

Your colours. Your language.

Match your wallpaper with Material You, or pick from six colours in light or dark. Synapse is fully translated into Hindi, and unlocks with your fingerprint or face.

Security model

We designed Synapse so we never have to be trusted.

Your master password turns into two separate keys on your phone. One proves who you are. The other unlocks your vault, and it never leaves the device.

// on your phone
masterKey = Argon2id(password, salt, 64 MiB, t=3)
authKey   = HKDF-SHA256(masterKey, "auth")
kek       = HKDF-SHA256(masterKey, "kek")
vaultKey  = random 256 bits, wrapped with kek
item      = AES-256-GCM(vaultKey, data,
              nonce = random 96 bits, aad = item id)

// what reaches the server
Argon2id(authKey), wrapped vaultKey, ciphertext

Server can see

Your username, linked Google or GitHub account, device names, and how many encrypted items you have.

Server can never see

Your master password, vault key, passwords, usernames, websites, notes, cards, 2FA secrets or passkeys.

Biometric unlock

Protected by a hardware Android Keystore key that's destroyed if your fingerprints or face change.

Encrypted sharing

X25519 key pairs per user. Items are sealed to each recipient; revoking access rotates the key.

Questions

What if I forget my master password?+

If you set up a recovery kit, you can reset it with your recovery key. Without one, nobody can open your vault, including us. Set up the kit in Settings → Account.

Why sign in with Google or GitHub as well?+

They identify your account for sync and sharing. Your master password is a separate layer that encrypts the vault, so someone who takes over your Google account still can't read anything.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. Your encrypted vault is stored on your phone and unlocks there. Changes sync when you're back online.

Why isn't it on Google Play?+

It's coming. Until then, the APK is signed with the Synapse release key and each release lists its SHA-256 checksum so you can verify it.

How do I delete my account?+

Settings → Account → Delete account, confirmed with your master password. It removes your account and vault from the server and the phone, and can delete your Drive backups too.
