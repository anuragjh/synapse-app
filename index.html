Your digital identity, securely connected.

Synapse is a password manager for Android. Your passwords, cards and notes are encrypted on your phone with a key only your master password can unlock. The Synapse server only ever stores encrypted data.

Encrypted on your device

AES-256-GCM encryption with an Argon2id-derived key. Nothing readable leaves your phone.

Works offline

Your vault lives on your device and syncs across devices when you're online.

Autofill and passkeys

Fill logins in apps and websites and keep two-factor codes next to your passwords.

Secure sharing

Share a copy, or share live with read or write access, and revoke it any time.

Security insights

Find weak, reused and exposed passwords.

Optional Google Drive backup

Encrypted backups in a hidden app folder of your own Drive. Off unless you turn it on.

Signing in

You sign in with Google or GitHub to identify your account. Your vault is protected by a separate master password that never leaves your device. Biometric unlock is available.
