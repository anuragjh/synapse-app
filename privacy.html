Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Version 2

The short version. Your vault is encrypted on your phone with a key only your master password can unlock. We store the encrypted result and a small amount of account data needed to sync it. We can't read your passwords, we don't sell anything, and the app contains no ads, trackers or analytics.

1. Who we are

Synapse is a password manager for Android, developed and operated by Aman Jha ("Synapse", "we", "us"). This policy covers the Synapse Android app, the Synapse sync service, and this website. For questions or requests, see Contact.

2. How Synapse is built to protect you

Synapse is designed so that we don't need to be trusted with your secrets:

  • Your master password never leaves your device. It is stretched with Argon2id (64 MiB of memory, 3 passes) and split with HKDF-SHA256 into two independent keys.
  • One key (the authentication key) proves who you are. The server stores only an Argon2id hash of it.
  • The other key (the key-encryption key) never leaves the device. It unlocks a random 256-bit vault key, which encrypts each vault item separately with AES-256-GCM.
  • Because of this, we cannot read, reset or recover your vault, and neither can anyone who gains access to our servers.

4. Data that stays on your device

Some data never leaves your phone: your master password, the decrypted vault while it's unlocked, your biometric unlock key (held in the Android Keystore, which we can't extract), app settings, and the password-health analysis (weak, reused and old password checks run entirely on the device).

5. Google user data

Synapse requests these Google permissions:

  • Sign-in (openid, email, profile): used only to sign you in and link your Google account to your Synapse account.
  • Google Drive app data folder (drive.appdata): requested only when you turn on "Back up to Google Drive". Synapse uses it to create, list, download and delete its own encrypted backup files in a hidden folder of your Drive. Synapse can't see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Drive backups are encrypted on your device before upload, so neither Google nor Synapse can read them. They go directly from your phone to Google and never pass through Synapse servers.

Synapse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use Google user data for advertising, don't sell it, don't use it to train AI models, and don't let people read it except where you ask us to for support, it's needed for security, or the law requires it.

7. Sharing between users

When you share an item, it is encrypted on your device to each recipient's public key. We store the encrypted item and who has access, so we can deliver it and enforce permissions, but we can't read it. People you share with can see the item's contents, and with edit access they can change it.

8. What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade personal data
  • Show ads, or include ad, tracking or analytics SDKs in the app
  • Use your data to train AI models
  • Build profiles of you

9. Security

Besides end-to-end encryption, we use HTTPS for all traffic, rate-limit sign-in and recovery attempts, store session tokens only as hashes, keep secrets out of source code, and run the service in isolated containers. No system is perfectly secure, so the design aims to keep your vault safe even if the server is compromised.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Access and export: view your account, devices and activity in Settings → Account, and export your vault (encrypted or as CSV) in Settings → Backup & transfer.
  • Correction: change your username, unlink providers and change your master password in the app.
  • Deletion: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all associated server data, confirmed with your master password. You can also choose to delete your Google Drive backups at the same time.
  • Revoking Google access: remove Synapse at myaccount.google.com/connections. This also deletes its hidden Drive data.

Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or the EU GDPR may give you further rights, including to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise any of them.

11. Children

Synapse isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our server and the third-party services above may process data in countries other than yours. Vault contents are always end-to-end encrypted wherever they're stored.

13. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change, and change the version and date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue or message the developer on LinkedIn. See also our Terms of service.
